## 🇨🇳 中文

把创意、剧本、源文档或参考素材，推进到已复核的原创温暖手绘奇幻动画成片。流程从 STEP 0 开始：锁定方向、范围、输入与制作条件；随后依次确定故事方向与前提大纲、建立角色/场景/道具/机械锚点、规划运动连续性与镜头表、产出文本（可选视觉）故事板、按参考绑定生成镜头、合成声音并做最终 QC，最后可选产出封面或海报。所有创意审批都使用选择卡，而不是用聊天里的「回复继续」代替确认。视觉上可采用水彩或水粉环境、赛璐珞层次、自然微运动、日常温情、旅途与成长、轻奇幻或机械诗意。它有明确的原创边界：可迁移手绘线稿、水彩环境、赛璐珞层次等特征，但不得复刻现有作品中的可识别角色、标志性服装、特定载具、具体地点、宗教建筑、Logo、文字标识或经典构图。

### ✨ 能做什么

- STEP 0–1 先锁定方向、范围、输入与制作条件，再产出故事方向、前提与故事大纲
- STEP 2 建立可复用的角色、场景、道具与机械锚点
- STEP 3 规划运动与连续性 → 输出可执行镜头表
- STEP 4 生成文本故事板，可选视觉故事板 → 确认后才生成
- STEP 5–6 按参考绑定生成镜头 → 合成声音并复核身份、动作、物理、音频与画面瑕疵
- 全程用选择卡做创意审批，STEP 7 可选输出封面或海报 → 不用「回复继续」代替确认

## 🇬🇧 English

Takes an idea, script, source document or reference set through to a reviewed, original warm hand-drawn fantasy animation short. It starts at STEP 0 by locking direction, scope, inputs and production constraints, then moves through story direction and premise outline, reusable character, environment, prop and machinery anchors, movement and continuity planning with a shot table, a text storyboard (optional visual storyboard), reference-bound clip generation, assembly with sound and final QC, and optionally a cover or poster. Every creative approval uses a choice card rather than a chat "reply to continue" confirmation. Visually it can use watercolor or gouache settings, cel-like layers, natural micro-movement, everyday tenderness, travel and growth, gentle fantasy or poetic machinery. Its originality boundary is explicit: transferable traits like hand-drawn line work and watercolor settings are fair game, while recognizable characters, signature costumes, specific vehicles, real places, religious buildings, logos, signage and iconic compositions must not be copied.

### ✨ What it does

- STEPS 0-1 lock direction, scope, inputs and production constraints, then produce story direction, premise and outline
- STEP 2 builds reusable character, environment, prop and machinery anchors
- STEP 3 plans movement and continuity → outputs an executable shot table
- STEP 4 writes a text storyboard with optional visual storyboard → generation waits for approval
- STEPS 5-6 generate reference-bound clips → assemble with sound and QC identity, motion, physics, audio and artifacts
- Choice cards gate every creative approval; STEP 7 optionally produces a cover or poster → no "reply to continue" shortcuts
